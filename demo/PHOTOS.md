# Demo photos

All 58 photos stored in the demo, plus one remote image, come from the
[NASA Image and Video Library](https://images.nasa.gov) (`images.nasa.gov`, searched through its API
at `images-api.nasa.gov`).

## Licenses

- NASA material is generally not subject to copyright in the United States. See the
  [NASA media usage guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). NASA asks
  for credit; the credit of each photo is listed below and in `src/data/photos.ts`.
- Photos credited to **ESA/Hubble** are published by ESA under the
  [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) license: they can
  be used with that credit.
- Third-party enhanced versions under non-commercial licenses (for example PIA21973 and PIA22946,
  processed by citizen scientists) were left out on purpose.

Credits come from the NASA description of each photo; when it gives none, the NASA center that
published it is shown.

## Processing

Each file is the `~orig` version linked in the table, converted to JPEG (quality 85) with `sips` and
reduced to 2560 px on the long edge when it was bigger. Smaller originals were kept at their size,
never enlarged. The page shows resized and cropped AVIF versions generated by Astro; the zoom opens
the whole file as AVIF. The Blue Marble, served as a plain file from `public/`, also has a 640 px copy
for the page (`-640.jpg`, resized with sharp).
